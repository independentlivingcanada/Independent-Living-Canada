Accessibility

Accessibility statement

Independent Living Canada aims to make its website and support pathways usable by older adults, people with disabilities and family supporters.

Our target

We are working toward WCAG 2.2 Level AA. We have not yet completed an independent accessibility audit and do not claim conformance.

Testing required before launch

  • Keyboard-only navigation and visible focus across all interactive controls.
  • Screen-reader review of headings, landmarks, form labels, errors, status messages and tabs.
  • Text resizing to 200%, zoom/reflow at narrow viewport widths, and horizontal overflow checks.
  • Text and non-text contrast, focus indicators, target size and motion preferences.
  • Accessible name, role, state and error announcements for authentication, request and provider forms.
  • Mobile testing on Android and iOS, including assistive technologies.
  • Alternative ways to request assistance for people who cannot use the website.

Service accessibility

Provider capabilities must be described precisely. A vehicle or home service must not be labelled accessible until the relevant dimensions, equipment, assistance limits and conditions have been verified. Users must be able to ask questions and decline an option without pressure.

Feedback and contact

A monitored accessibility feedback contact and response process must be added before public launch. This draft is not yet a monitored feedback channel.
