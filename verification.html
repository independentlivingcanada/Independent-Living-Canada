Our trust framework

How provider review should work

A transparent, evidence-based process designed to help people compare services without overstating what has been checked.

Review stages

1

Identity and business

Confirm legal operating name, business contact details, service address or service area, and responsible representative.

2

Safety and qualifications

Check applicable licences/permits, insurance, driver/vehicle requirements, relevant training, screening and references for the services offered.

3

Service fit and accountability

Document exact services, exclusions, accessibility capacity, geographic coverage, pricing, complaints contact and incident process.

Evidence and status labels

  • Invited: an invitation was sent; no participation agreement is implied.
  • Responded: the organization replied; interest or eligibility is not implied.
  • Documents received: documents were supplied but not necessarily validated.
  • Verification in progress: required checks are being completed; not a verified listing.
  • Verified for listed services: required checks were completed and dated for the specific service scope.
  • Expired / suspended: checks need renewal or a concern requires review; listing must not be promoted.
Important distinction: “Verified” does not mean government-approved, endorsed, funded, contracted, clinically regulated or guaranteed safe. Funding administrators decide whether a particular client, service and provider arrangement qualifies.

Ongoing review

  • Record evidence source, reviewer, review date, expiry date and limitations.
  • Recheck insurance and time-limited credentials before expiry.
  • Require prompt disclosure of material changes, complaints, incidents and service suspensions.
  • Provide a correction/appeal route and a process to remove or suspend a profile.
  • Never sell verification status or allow payment to change review outcomes.

Before public provider listings begin, this framework must be approved operationally and reviewed against applicable legal, privacy and safeguarding obligations.
