## Verification checklist before any provider is approved

1. Confirm legal identity and authorized representative.
2. Verify current liability and commercial auto insurance appropriate to the exact service.
3. Verify driver screening, training, vehicle inspections, wheelchair securement and passenger-assistance procedures for rides.
4. Verify trade licences, permits, insurance, qualifications and references for accessibility contractors.
5. Verify references, safeguarding, complaint handling, incident reporting and worker screening appropriate to role.
6. Agree written service boundaries: no emergency, ambulance/stretcher, medical transport, nursing, medication, personal care or clinical tasks.
7. Confirm prices, cancellation terms, service area, hours, capacity, accessibility limitations and how a customer can decline.
8. Obtain written provider agreement and document reviewer, date, evidence reviewed, outcome and renewal date.
9. Only then activate relevant provider service categories in the staff portal.
