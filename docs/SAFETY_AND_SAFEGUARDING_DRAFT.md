# Safety and safeguarding operating standard — draft

**Status:** Development draft for legal, insurance and safeguarding review. It is not a certification or completed operating policy.

## Non-negotiable service boundaries

The initial platform may consider ordinary non-emergency accessible rides, companionship/social visits, errands/groceries, light household help and home accessibility modifications. It does not provide emergency response, ambulance or stretcher transport, medical transport, clinical/nursing care, medication administration, personal care, lifting/transfers or regulated health services.

If a request appears urgent or medical, do not match it. Direct emergencies to 911 and non-emergency health questions to an appropriate regulated provider or public service.

## Consent and supported decision-making

- Confirm the person receiving the service understands and agrees to the request and the involvement of any family supporter.
- A family member's account or payment does not automatically authorize decisions for another adult.
- Record the recipient's consent and scope of the supporter's authority where relevant; collect only what is necessary.
- Allow the person receiving support to decline a provider, cancel, or change arrangements without pressure.

## Provider screening

Before approval, record:
- Legal identity, business registration where applicable and authorized contact.
- References and role-appropriate background screening.
- Insurance certificates and policy limits relevant to each service.
- Applicable licences, permits, trade credentials and vehicle/driver requirements.
- Training, accessibility capabilities, service limits and emergency procedures.
- Written code of conduct, privacy expectations, safeguarding and complaint procedures.
- Renewal dates and evidence reviewer's identity.

For rides, verify commercial auto coverage, driver eligibility, vehicle inspection, wheelchair securement, passenger assistance boundaries, seatbelt practices and clear limitations for mobility devices. Do not infer accessibility from a provider's marketing.

For home modifications, verify contractor qualifications, insurance, permits, references, scope and warranties. Do not provide engineering, medical or building-code determinations through the platform.

## Safeguarding and conduct

- No harassment, discrimination, financial exploitation, coercion, gifts/loans, or use of a customer's bank card or PIN.
- No unapproved additional passengers or workers.
- No entry to a home without the customer's informed agreement.
- Keep communications and service arrangements transparent.
- Do not photograph, record or share a customer's home or personal details without explicit, documented permission.
- Minimize collection of disability and health information; do not request diagnoses or medical records.

## Incident and complaint handling

1. Address immediate danger first; call 911 where appropriate.
2. Make sure the affected person is safe and has a trusted contact if they want one.
3. Preserve relevant facts without speculation; record date/time, parties, actions and outcome.
4. Escalate safeguarding allegations to the designated lead immediately.
5. Temporarily suspend a provider where credible safety concerns require it; do not wait for a routine review.
6. Notify affected people and authorities where legally required, after appropriate advice.
7. Document investigation, corrective action, appeal/review and closure.
8. Track patterns such as missed rides, late arrivals, falls, property damage, complaints and cancellations.

## Operational launch requirements

- Name a safeguarding lead and backup with monitored contact channels.
- Establish incident severity levels and response-time targets.
- Obtain legal/insurance review of the platform's role and provider contracts.
- Publish complaint, incident, privacy, accessibility and cancellation processes.
- Train staff and providers and record completion.
- Test the staff portal, audit trail, provider suspension and request escalation.
- Run a supervised manual pilot with no automated matching.

## Open decisions

- Final legal entity and contracting model.
- Insurance coverage for the platform's intermediary role.
- Privacy impact assessment and retention/deletion schedule.
- Background check standards by role and province.
- Incident reporting obligations and regulator notification rules.
- Accessibility audit and accommodation process.
