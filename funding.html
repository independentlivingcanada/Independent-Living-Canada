Manitoba funding guide

Explore ways to make support more affordable.

Some Manitobans may qualify for publicly funded home care, self/family-managed care or home accessibility assistance. Eligibility and approved services depend on an assessment and each program's rules.

Manitoba Home Care

Home care is available to eligible Manitoba residents of any age who need health services or help with daily activities. A Regional Health Authority (RHA) assesses needs and determines the type and amount of service.

How to start: Contact your RHA Home Care intake office and request an assessment.

Official Manitoba Home Care information

🤝

Self/Family-Managed Care

For eligible Home Care clients, this option can provide funding to arrange assessed non-professional home-care services. The WRHA confirms that a self/family manager may engage an agency or employ staff, subject to its assessment, agreement and program rules. Other RHAs administer their own arrangements; confirm local terms.

How to start: Ask your Home Care case coordinator whether self/family-managed care is appropriate.

WRHA program details

🛠️

Safe and Healthy Home for Seniors

Eligible older adults may qualify for assistance with essential home adaptations. The program is administered by March of Dimes Canada; funding, eligible work, income rules and application windows must be confirmed directly.

How to start: Review current eligibility and application information before hiring a contractor.

Program information and applications

🚌

Accessible community transportation

Manitoba's Mobility Disadvantaged Transportation Program supports eligible municipal/community transportation initiatives. It is not a general individual subsidy for any private ride.

How to start: Ask your municipality or local accessible transportation organization what services operate in your area.

Know the difference

Funding is not the same as provider approval

1

Publicly delivered service

The RHA arranges or provides services under its Home Care program after assessment.

2

Managed-care funding

An eligible client or family manager arranges assessed supports using funding and program rules. Confirm whether the provider and service qualify before committing.

3

Grant or reimbursement

A separate program may help with a defined purchase or adaptation. Approval, eligible expenses and payment timing vary.

Important: Manitoba Health says it does not license private home-care companies as a special category. A business appearing in this directory does not mean it is government-approved, contracted, insured, credentialed or eligible for a particular funding program. Ask the provider and funding administrator to confirm these points in writing.

Before choosing a provider

Questions to ask

  • Do you accept clients using Self/Family-Managed Care funding in my RHA?
  • Which exact services can you provide under the client's approved care plan?
  • Can you provide invoices, service records and documentation required by the funding manager?
  • What are your rates, minimum hours, cancellation terms and travel charges?
  • Do you carry appropriate insurance, complete relevant background checks and have staff training for the services offered?
  • Can you confirm in writing that the funding administrator permits the proposed arrangement?

Regional contacts

Ask your Regional Health Authority

Eligibility, assessments, service levels and self/family-managed care arrangements are administered regionally. Start with the official provincial Home Care page for the correct regional contact. In Winnipeg, the WRHA Home Care Intake Line is 204-788-8330.
