Privacy

Privacy notice

Independent Living Canada is a development project intended to connect older adults, people with disabilities and family supporters with practical local support. This notice describes the current pilot design and must be reviewed and completed before public launch.

Information the development site may collect

  • Account email address and authentication identifiers.
  • Service request category, city/community, neighbourhood, description, preferred date/time, accessibility logistics and recipient name if provided.
  • Requester contact name, email, phone and preferred contact method, stored separately from the general request record.
  • Provider application contact details, service description, service categories and service area.
  • Consent confirmation and timestamp associated with a submitted request.

Do not submit sensitive information

Do not provide diagnoses, medication lists, health-card numbers, medical records, financial account details, government identification numbers, passwords or unnecessary disability/health details. Do not use the site for emergencies or clinical/personal care.

Where information is processed

The static website is hosted through GitHub Pages. The application's authentication and database are hosted in a separate Supabase project configured in Canada Central (Toronto). The site also loads decorative photographs from Unsplash. Hosting and subprocessors must be re-confirmed before launch.

How information is used

In the future, submitted information would be used to review support requests, assess provider applications, coordinate appropriate options and communicate with the requester. Provider applications are not public listings and are not approved automatically. The development pilot does not currently provide public matching or bookings.

Access and safeguards

Account-level database access is restricted by row-level security. Private contact details are stored separately. A restricted staff portal uses a server-side staff allowlist and logs administrative status changes. These controls still require end-to-end testing before real information is accepted.

Retention, access requests and complaints

A final retention schedule, deletion process, privacy contact, complaint channel and response timelines have not yet been approved. These must be completed and published before public launch. Do not rely on this draft as a channel for submitting privacy requests.

Changes and legal review

This draft is not legal advice and is not a representation that the service complies with every applicable privacy law. The final policy must be reviewed for applicable federal and Manitoba requirements, including any requirements arising from the exact services and providers involved.
